THE POOLROOMS

A quiet, procedurally assembled maze of white-tiled halls, sunken pools, and endless corridors.

Pools are real basins with sloped, terraced floors. Wading through water slows you by 20%. Some pools are far deeper than they look. In deep water, hold Space to swim, or let go and sink. Stay under too long and your vision fades.

Controls: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, C to crouch (hold), mouse to look, Space to jump (hold Space in deep water to swim), F to toggle flashlight.
